# ALPHA-002 Completion Evidence

## Implemented boundary

ALPHA-002 converts each immutable ALPHA-001 selected question into one ordinary
Hermes task. A dedicated VM1 identity may lease only
`alpha_research_execution`, only for `bulletproof_bt`, only at risk zero. The task
binds the campaign, selected candidate, normalized question, DATA build and content
digest, full Bulletproof commit, admitted Parquet path, finite trial budget and
Research Intelligence citations.

Bulletproof verifies the checkout, panel bytes and question digest before work. An
exact registered hypothesis ID or exact title executes one prospectively registered
variant in the classic engine. An unsupported question instead produces a cited
strategy-engineering requirement, reports `strategy_generation`, and records zero
trials. It is never mapped by semantic similarity.

The native path writes point-in-time representation, market-model and search-plan
contracts, validates experiment truth, evaluates the final 20% temporal holdout,
doubles observed cost drag, finalizes an atomic run bundle, retains that bundle
outside the disposable worktree and precommits a digest-bound Bulletproof memory
receipt. Hermes resumes the BT-009 registry, canonical evidence, publication,
projection and memory stages idempotently.

## Operations and safety

The executor heartbeats every 30 seconds, runs under an unprivileged systemd service,
has a six-hour ceiling and at most three task attempts. Native data and the primary
checkout are read-only. Cancellation propagates to the task; terminal crashes become
visible campaign `needs_attention` states. Mission Control and Telegram expose task,
lease, attempts, heartbeat, disposition and failure.

All capital, order, self-approval and production-promotion authority fields are
false. A candidate is only eligible for later shadow review. The two publication
reviews are distinct deterministic registry checks, not a claim of full AGT-006
provider/context independence; any later promotion must bind a real AGT-006 receipt.
