## Scope delivered

RI-004 turns RI-001 canonical evidence and RI-003 retrieval into durable
institutional memory without granting that memory decision authority.

- Opposition records classify direct contradiction, boundary failure, replication
  failure, method critique, alternative explanation, and operational contradiction.
  Direct and replication contradictions require explicit material comparability.
- Reviews append a digest-bound record that supersedes the proposal. They do not
  rewrite it, and a proposal cannot be reviewed twice.
- Outcome records distinguish valid negative evidence from invalid attempts. Invalid
  attempts require a named failure mechanism and search returns the classes separately.
- Beliefs are canonical evidence objects with typed assessments, declared population
  and horizon, uncertainty, supporting and opposing evidence, dependencies, minority
  assessments, review dates, invalidation conditions, and immutable supersession.
  Probabilistic beliefs additionally require an event and calibration basis.
- Episodes publish shared canonical memory linking inputs, outputs, decisions, tools,
  failures, alternatives, surprises, lessons, and new questions. Episodes contain no
  approval capability.
- Dossiers freeze the exact claim, support, opposition, belief, outcome, contradiction,
  and episode identities available at a declared cutoff. Included scientific objects
  retain content digests, source coordinates, and RI-003 citation replay paths.
- Protected objects remain visible as redacted existence records with object identity
  and access-denial reason; protected content and digest are not disclosed.
- Replay never rewrites the frozen dossier. It reports missing, digest-changed, or
  superseded objects as later impacts on the retained historical state.
- Mission Control presents a read-only dossier list and inspector with support,
  opposition, dissent, redactions, synthesis, recommendation, and replay impacts.
- A typed asynchronous client supplies authenticated dossier reads and redacts its
  bearer token from bounded errors.

## Governance and security boundary

Dossiers are evidence products, not approvals. No RI-004 endpoint materializes tasks,
changes approval state, or executes commands. Mission Control adds only authenticated
GET proxies for dossier detail and replay. Creating institutional records remains an
authenticated orchestrator operation subject to the existing access context.

No protected content is copied into a lower-access dossier. Redaction retains only the
fact that evidence exists, its canonical object ID, and why access was denied. Search
re-authorizes outcome evidence, and dossier reads enforce project and access class.

## Rollback and limitations

The additive downgrade removes only the three RI-004 tables: opposition records,
outcome records, and dossiers. Beliefs and episodes are canonical RI-001 evidence
objects and must be retained if RI-004 has been used; operators must export or preserve
institutional records before any production downgrade.

RI-004 does not automatically discover contradictions, backfill legacy evidence,
approve recommendations, promote research, or establish production ingestion/query
SLOs. Corpus security, backups, projection recovery, scale qualification, and automated
surveillance remain RI-005 and RI-006 work.
