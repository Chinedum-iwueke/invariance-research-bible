## Scope delivered

RI-006 adds governed current awareness without giving publications, connectors, or
models research-admission authority.

- A strict source registry binds project, feed identity, HTTPS URL and host allowlist,
  domains, rights, access class, cadence, freshness objective, owner, and enabled state.
- The initial registry contains two curator-owned quantitative-finance primary feeds:
  arXiv q-fin Trading and Market Microstructure and arXiv q-fin Portfolio Management.
  Only public metadata and abstracts are in connector scope; full text remains subject
  to artifact-specific rights and the RI-002 ingestion boundary.
- A bounded Atom/RSS connector rejects redirects, non-HTTPS or non-allowlisted hosts,
  oversized responses, DTD/entity declarations, and more than 500 entries per poll.
- The scheduler retries connection, timeout, and 5xx collection failures at most three
  times with bounded exponential delay. It records an explicit failed receipt instead
  of treating an outage as a week with no publications.
- Immutable fetch receipts bind source, connector version, fetch time, response status,
  entry identities, counts and a SHA-256 digest. Exact repeated receipts are idempotent.
- Publication records preserve corrections and retractions as new versions linked to
  prior records. Duplicates are counted without creating a second candidate.
- Abstracts and titles are untrusted data. Instruction, tool, secret-exfiltration and
  approval-spoofing text is rejected before candidate publication.
- Deterministic novelty compares each candidate against both same-project canonical RI
  retrieval projections and prior surveillance records. Assessments record the corpus
  basis, nearest-prior text digest, novelty, evidence quality, applicability domains,
  limitations and mandatory contradiction review.
- Technique briefs and proposed questions are routing artifacts only. They explicitly
  carry `approval_required=true`, `execution_authority=false` and no auto-adoption or
  auto-trial capability.
- Candidate dispositions are append-only, digest-bound routing events attributable to
  a research authority. Weekly digests retain citation provenance, correction and
  retraction counts, scores, routes, and the non-authority boundary.
- Mission Control displays approved feeds, candidates and weekly digests and provides
  read-only citation replay. A one-cycle command and operator runbook support later
  deployment behind an externally managed timer.

## Rollback and limitations

The additive downgrade removes only RI-006 source, receipt, publication, routing-event
and digest tables. It does not alter canonical evidence, ingestion, retrieval,
institutional memory, backups or recovery evidence. Export RI-006 audit records before
downgrading after production use.

RI-006 does not deploy a production timer, prove live source availability or rate-limit
behavior, ingest full text, infer scientific truth from abstracts, automatically find
all contradictions, approve research programs, register trials, or replace the Canon
Curator and research authority. Initial lexical novelty is deterministic and auditable;
its quality must be evaluated on observed candidates before a learned model is added.
