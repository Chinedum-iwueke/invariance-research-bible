## Boundary tests

When ownership is unclear, apply these tests:

1. **Why must the institution do this?** Book I.
2. **Who may decide, approve, veto, or change state?** Book II.
3. **How is evidence recovered, represented, retrieved, remembered, or defended?** Book III.
4. **How are questions, hypotheses, search, and falsification organized?** Book IV.
5. **How is a historical experiment executed and evidenced deterministically?** Book V.
6. **How is a learned estimator or policy formulated and evaluated?** Book VI.
7. **How is capital risk admitted, combined, constrained, and scaled?** Book VII.
8. **How do venues, orders, liquidity, and execution behave?** Book VIII.
9. **How are replaceable agents constructed, equipped, and evaluated?** Book IX.
10. **How are all requirements implemented, deployed, tested, and operated?** Book X.
