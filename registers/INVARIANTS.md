# Constitutional Invariants Reconciliation Register

**Status:** Controlled reconciliation baseline  
**Authority:** Book I Founder’s Edition, later architectural decisions in `PROJECT_CONTEXT_FOR_CODEX.md`, and adopted ADRs

The Founder’s Edition lists fifteen invariants. Version 2 preserves their substantive commitments while distinguishing constitutional identity from scientific heuristics, empirical expectations, and operating priorities. No entry is silently deleted.

| FE invariant | Classification | Controlled Version 2 treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Reality is the final authority | Constitutional | Evidence about reality can be incomplete or erroneous; claims change through governed review, not raw observation alone. |
| Science precedes trading | Constitutional | Scientific integrity precedes commercial pressure; trading and prospective operation also produce evidence and do not “validate science” by profit alone. |
| Discovery precedes optimization | Constitutional default | Questions, anomalies, and registered hypotheses precede performance optimization; safety and implementation optimization may be required independently. |
| Institutional memory is sacred | Constitutional | Every material record returns to Research Intelligence subject to provenance, retention, privacy, security, and lawful deletion controls. |
| Falsification precedes confirmation | Scientific principle | Designs actively seek disconfirming and discriminating evidence; no simplistic sequence substitutes for appropriate inference. |
| Representation determines understanding | Scientific principle | Representation constrains what can be learned and must be explicit; it does not uniquely determine understanding. |
| Information is more fundamental than profit | Mission principle | Reliable knowledge is the institutional product; economic viability remains a necessary operational constraint, not proof of knowledge. |
| Search is the institution’s scarcest resource | Operating principle | Attention, protected data access, time, compute, and capital are jointly constrained; search is governed allocation, not metaphysically singular. |
| Every belief is provisional | Epistemic principle | Empirical and architectural beliefs are revisable. Constitutional commitments change only through explicit canonical governance. |
| Weak edges compound into durable advantage | Research heuristic | Modest independent contributions may combine; dependence, costs, estimation error, decay, and capacity can prevent compounding. No durability is guaranteed. |
| Machine learning serves scientific reasoning | Constitutional boundary | ML supports representation, estimation, calibration, uncertainty, and policies; it is never evidentiary or governance authority by itself. |
| Governance protects intelligence | Constitutional boundary | Exploration, evaluation, governance, and promotion authority remain separated; no actor promotes its own work. |
| Learning never ends | Mission principle | The institution remains open to correction and continued inquiry; completion of bounded work remains required and measurable. |
| Markets are teachers before opportunities | Ethical/scientific stance | Market evidence informs learning; anthropomorphic language does not replace explicit observation and causal discipline. |
| The institution is greater than any individual or model | Constitutional boundary | Institutional authority, memory, and continuity remain external to replaceable humans, agents, models, and vendors while accountable human authority stays explicit. |

## Later canonical invariants requiring explicit inclusion

The project context adds commitments that the Founder’s list did not state precisely enough:

1. Research Intelligence is the institutional brain, corpus, and long-term memory; Hermes is the governed operating system and organization; Bulletproof BT is the deterministic laboratory.
2. Model runtimes are replaceable; deterministic services remain authoritative for simulation, calculation, risk, state transitions, and primary evidence.
3. Constitutional, Scientific, Institutional, and Operational Knowledge retain distinct authority and provenance.
4. Architecture and research rigor improve process but do not guarantee profitable alpha.
5. Representation precedes prediction; anomaly and scientific question precede optimization.
6. Hypotheses are registered and exposed to falsification before privileged evaluation.
7. Backtest, robustness, shadow, execution replay, demo, micro-live, and production are distinct evidentiary environments.
8. Crypto is the initial model organism while institutional contracts remain market-agnostic.
9. Exploration, evaluation, governance, and promotion authority remain separated.
10. Every material experiment, failure, deployment, incident, and engineering task returns knowledge to Research Intelligence.

These additions are clarifications and architectural commitments, not evidence that the corresponding systems already exist.
