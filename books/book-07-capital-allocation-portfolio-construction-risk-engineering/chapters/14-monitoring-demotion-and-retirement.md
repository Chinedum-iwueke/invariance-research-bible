# Chapter 14 — Monitoring, Demotion, and Retirement

Portfolio monitoring compares forecasts, risk, execution, constraints, and operations with their authorized contracts. It supports action; it is not a passive dashboard.

## 14.1 Monitoring layers

Track exposure and constraints in real time; P&L, costs, margin, liquidity, and reconciliation at operational cadence; forecast calibration and support as outcomes mature; covariance, concentration, capacity, and scenario risk periodically; and model or strategy evidence on scheduled review.

## 14.2 Attribution before action

Diagnose forecast failure, changed opportunity, cost increase, capacity breach, dependency shift, model drift, execution defect, data issue, or random variation. Immediate survival actions need not await diagnosis, but restoration does.

## 14.3 Demotion ladder

Responses include alert, investigate, freeze risk increases, reduce budget, restrict instruments, shadow only, quarantine, retire, or emergency liquidate. Each trigger specifies authority and whether it is automatic.

## 14.4 Retirement

Retirement preserves artifacts, incidents, decisions, dependencies, and lessons. Open positions and orders follow an exit plan. Consumers and upstream services are notified. Identifiers are never recycled.

## 14.5 Re-entry

A retired or quarantined candidate returns through a new evidence package and explicit decision. A favorable short rebound does not reset history.

> **Risk rule B7-RK14 — Restoration requires more evidence than reduction**  
> Risk can be reduced immediately under safety authority; restoring or increasing it requires diagnosis, satisfied conditions, and an attributable decision.
