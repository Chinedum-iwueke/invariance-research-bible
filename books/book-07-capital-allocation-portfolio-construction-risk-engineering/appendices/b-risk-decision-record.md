# Appendix B — Risk Decision Record

The record contains decision and replay identity; canonical timestamp; portfolio, position, cash, collateral, order, price, FX, and margin snapshots; forecasts and uncertainty; covariance, tail, scenario, cost, and capacity versions; objective; hard and soft constraints; solver status; active constraints; continuous and rounded targets; rejected alternatives; fallback; authorization; and output digests.
