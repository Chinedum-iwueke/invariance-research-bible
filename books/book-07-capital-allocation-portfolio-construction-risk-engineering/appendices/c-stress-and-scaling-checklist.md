# Appendix C — Stress and Scaling Checklist

Before scaling, verify protected scientific evidence; forecast calibration and support; portfolio dependency; gross and net concentration; tail and drawdown risk; turnover, costs, capacity, and liquidation; leverage, margin, funding, and FX; scenario and reverse stress; data and operational health; shadow or micro-live parity; staged limits; monitoring; kill and rollback; accountable approval; conditions; review date; and expiry.
