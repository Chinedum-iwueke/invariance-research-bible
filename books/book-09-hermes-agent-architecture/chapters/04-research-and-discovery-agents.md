# Chapter 4 — Research and Discovery Agents

Research agents retrieve evidence, map questions, propose hypotheses, construct experiments, analyze artifacts, and document uncertainty. Their output enters the governed discovery loop of Book IV.

## 4.1 Charters

Each task states question, scope, sources, protected evidence, methods, trial and compute budgets, tools, stopping, outputs, reviewer, and forbidden actions. Exploratory and confirmatory work remain labeled.

## 4.2 Work products

Agents create source dossiers, claim-evidence maps, competing mechanisms, hypothesis contracts, experiment specifications, analysis notebooks or reports, limitations, and closeout records. Every material claim links evidence or is marked proposal.

## 4.3 Tool use

Patterns that interleave language-model reasoning and actions have been studied in systems such as [ReAct](../../../bibliography/references.md#yao-et-al-2023-react), while [Toolformer](../../../bibliography/references.md#schick-et-al-2023-toolformer) explores learned API use. These papers demonstrate methods in evaluated settings; they do not establish safe institutional autonomy.

## 4.4 Stop conditions

Workers stop on completion, invalidity, futility, budget, safety, unavailable evidence, or authority boundary. They never redefine success to preserve a favorable result.

> **Agent rule B9-AG04 — Discovery workers preserve the search**  
> Every proposal, evidence exposure, material failed branch, amendment, and stopping decision required to interpret discovery remains attributable.
