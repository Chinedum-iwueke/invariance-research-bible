# Chapter 7 — Knowledge Organization Agents

Knowledge agents extract, normalize, link, deduplicate, summarize, and curate evidence under Book III. They improve access without becoming the canonical truth layer.

## 7.1 Tasks

Ingest sources, classify knowledge type, extract claims and entities, propose citations and graph edges, detect contradictions, map lineage, generate indexes, and flag stale or missing evidence.

A literature-surveillance agent executes Book III’s approved journal watchlists on daily or weekly schedules. It discovers new issues and articles, verifies source identity and rights, deduplicates versions, links corrections or retractions, retrieves permitted content, and compares claims and techniques with prior institutional knowledge. Its outputs are a coverage manifest, acquisition exceptions, a source-grounded digest, novelty and contradiction candidates, and routed technique proposals with named owners and deadlines.

The agent may identify a method worth testing and prepare a registration-ready brief. It may not represent publication as validation, silently alter an active strategy, authorize an experiment, promote a candidate, allocate capital, or deploy code. Application occurs only through the receiving laboratory’s deterministic tools, evidence requirements, review, and authority gates.

## 7.2 Publication

Machine extraction retains source spans, producer, confidence subtype, and validation status. Material claims require deterministic checks or review. Graphs and embeddings remain rebuildable projections.

## 7.3 Summaries

Summaries state scope, omitted evidence, disagreement, and source links. Compression never replaces primary artifacts or merges proposal with observation.

## 7.4 Access

Retrieval respects protected evidence, legal restrictions, and task purpose. An agent cannot quote access-controlled material into a lower-class artifact.

External browsing and subscription connectors receive destination allowlists, read-only credentials, rate and cost limits, content-size limits, schedule windows, and expiry. Source pages and documents are untrusted data, not instructions; embedded prompts, scripts, or requests cannot expand the agent’s task or capabilities.

> **Agent rule B9-AG07 — Curation preserves provenance**  
> Every machine-organized claim remains connected to its source, extraction, validation, knowledge class, and access policy.
