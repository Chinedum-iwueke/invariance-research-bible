# Chapter 15 — Research Intelligence Publication

The laboratory produces primary experimental evidence. Research Intelligence makes that evidence discoverable and comparable without altering its meaning.

## 15.1 Publication boundary

After a run bundle is finalized, validated, and assigned a disposition, a publisher emits an immutable evidence reference and derived searchable records. Publication does not mean promotion. Invalid, negative, inconclusive, and failed operational attempts can all be published under the correct class.

The target publication record includes experiment, trial, attempt, hypothesis, implementation, data and representation identities; validation status; artifact manifest and digest; schema compatibility; evidence environment; protected-data exposure; and producer timestamp.

## 15.2 Current extraction surfaces

The pinned repository can extract compact run and trade datasets from experiment directories, enrich rows from lightweight artifacts, record parse and completeness flags, and generate extraction diagnostics. It also contains trade-memory, artifact-query, lifecycle, research-copilot, and strategy-terminal contracts that can support downstream retrieval.

These are integration surfaces. The Book III canonical evidence store, concept graph, belief records, access controls, and citation services remain institutional targets outside the public repository audit.

## 15.3 Compatibility

Research Intelligence accepts a producer only when schema identity and semantic compatibility are known. It does not combine two columns named `r_multiple_net` if their risk denominator, cost treatment, or stop policy differ. Unknown compatibility produces separate cohorts.

Derived cross-run tables retain source row identity and extraction version. Re-extraction after a producer upgrade creates a new projection; it does not overwrite the earlier interpretation silently.

## 15.4 Query and comparison

Useful queries ask for comparable trials by hypothesis family, representation, data scope, execution profile, tier, and validation state. Similarity by strategy name is insufficient. Comparison services return limitations and incompatible dimensions alongside metrics.

The laboratory can publish structural buckets, decision traces, cost attribution, and truth reports that help diagnose why an effect appears. Research Intelligence connects those findings to prior mechanisms, failures, sources, and decisions.

## 15.5 Feedback

Publication creates new questions: missing fields, repeated invalidity, unexplained rejection clusters, cost sensitivity, regime boundaries, or contradictions with prior evidence. Those become governed discovery inputs in Book IV. They do not trigger autonomous promotion or live changes.

> **Laboratory rule B5-LR15 — Publication is typed, not celebratory**  
> Evidence enters institutional memory with its validity, environment, scope, and limitations intact, regardless of whether its headline metric is favorable.
