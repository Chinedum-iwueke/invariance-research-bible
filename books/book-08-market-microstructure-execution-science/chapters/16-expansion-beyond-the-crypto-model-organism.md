# Chapter 16 — Expansion Beyond the Crypto Model Organism

Crypto provides an initial test bed, not a template to rename for every market. Expansion preserves canonical intent while adding market-specific facts.

## 16.1 Foreign exchange

Add dealer and venue fragmentation, last-look and rejection, quote conventions, spot dates, swaps and rollover, sessions, holidays, lot and pip semantics, and credit relationships.

## 16.2 Equities

Add auctions, halts, market sessions, corporate actions, short sale and borrow, odd lots, routing obligations, off-exchange activity, and settlement.

## 16.3 Futures and options

Add expiry, rolls, delivery, multipliers, variation margin, calendars, spreads, option exercise and assignment, Greeks, volatility surfaces, and clearing.

## 16.4 Acceptance process

For each market, build instrument and venue schemas, rule packs, data audits, adapter certification, execution models, reconciliation, risk controls, replay, demo where available, and bounded prospective evidence. Crypto-specific defaults never fill unknown fields.

## 16.5 Completion

Book VIII is accepted when the institution can identify instruments, reconstruct time and state, model venue mechanics honestly, manage complete order lifecycles, measure costs and latency, reconcile adapters, stage evidence, kill unsafe mutation, calibrate simulation, monitor degradation, and expand without erasing market differences.

> **Execution rule B8-EX16 — Market agnosticism requires explicit specialization**  
> A portable execution architecture is built from stable canonical contracts plus verified market and venue extensions, not lowest-common-denominator assumptions.
