# Appendix C — Founder’s Edition Migration

The Founder’s Edition remains an immutable historical source. Version 2 revises its thirty chapters into sixteen constitutional chapters and relocates operational, cognitive, laboratory, model, portfolio, execution, agent, and implementation detail to the volumes that own those subjects.

The complete chapter-by-chapter disposition, page ranges, evidence notes, terminology conflicts, and relocation destinations are recorded in the [Book I Founder’s Edition chapter ledger](../../../migrations/BOOK-01-CHAPTER-LEDGER.md). The cross-volume migration policy appears in the [Books I–III migration plan](../../../migrations/BOOKS-01-03-MIGRATION-PLAN.md).

No omission from Version 2 implies that the source material was unread or discarded. Each Founder chapter is classified as retained, revised, split, relocated, consolidated, superseded, or archived. Later volumes must cite the ledger when assuming ownership of relocated material.
