# Appendix A — ML Problem Contract

Record identity and version; scientific role; population and unit; observation, decision, target, and evaluation times; information set; target and horizon; action or consumer; loss, metrics, utility, costs, and constraints; sample and weighting policy; censoring and missingness; dataset and split design; baselines; uncertainty and abstention; search budget; protected evidence; operational latency; monitoring; invalidity; and accountable owner and reviewer.

Changing population, target, horizon, information set, or action creates a new formulation version.
