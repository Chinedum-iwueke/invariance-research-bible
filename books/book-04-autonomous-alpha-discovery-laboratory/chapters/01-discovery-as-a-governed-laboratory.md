# Chapter 1 — Discovery as a Governed Laboratory

Discovery expands the set of questions, representations, mechanisms, and candidate relationships the institution can investigate. That flexibility creates scientific value and selection risk at the same time. The Autonomous Alpha Discovery Laboratory therefore treats search as a governed experiment whose attempts, adaptations, evidence access, and stopping conditions remain visible.

The laboratory is not an autonomous profit engine. It proposes and tests bounded scientific objects. Hermes supplies charters, budgets, registration, and authority. Research Intelligence supplies prior evidence and preserves outcomes. Bulletproof BT executes deterministic experiments. Accountable reviewers decide what follows.

## 1.1 The discovery object

A discovery is a justified change in the institution’s evidence map: a new question, supported relationship, weakened belief, diagnosed artifact, boundary condition, mechanism distinction, or useful negative result. A high-scoring backtest is an observation awaiting interpretation.

The laboratory operates over typed objects: observations, anomalies, questions, hypotheses, representations, methods, experiment families, results, critiques, and dispositions. Each object has identity and lineage. Search code, model output, and agent conversation are not substitutes.

> **Discovery rule B4-DR01 — Search is itself experimental evidence**  
> Every adaptive choice capable of influencing which result is reported belongs to the registered search history and the interpretation of that result.

## 1.2 The governed loop

The default loop is:

1. observe a deviation, contradiction, or knowledge gap;
2. retrieve nearest prior work, failures, and relevant theory;
3. formulate a scoped question;
4. propose mechanisms and alternatives;
5. register hypothesis, representation, search family, metrics, budgets, and evidence policy;
6. execute bounded search and deterministic experiments;
7. preserve every material attempt and privileged exposure;
8. evaluate with independent criticism;
9. close, revise, or form a candidate;
10. publish evidence and new questions to Research Intelligence.

Exploration can precede a confirmatory hypothesis, but its status remains exploratory. A result-generated hypothesis begins a new registered test rather than rewriting the earlier search.

## 1.3 Discovery and alpha

The laboratory can identify predictive edge. Realizable alpha requires later evidence about costs, capacity, constraints, execution, and portfolio contribution. Realized return is further downstream. Book IV does not promote scientific findings by calling them alpha early.

The name “Alpha Discovery Laboratory” describes institutional purpose, not the epistemic status of every output. Many valuable outputs will never become candidates.

## 1.4 Autonomy and authority

Autonomous workers operate under task charters specifying objective, markets, data, representations, tools, trial and compute budgets, protected evidence, elapsed time, stopping, outputs, and forbidden actions. They may choose tactics within the charter and must request amendment when the useful next action exceeds it.

No worker expands its own holdout access, hides failed trials, evaluates its own promotion, or allocates resources beyond the charter. Different agent names do not establish independent review.

## 1.5 Failure and closeout

Every session ends as completed, invalid, futile, budget-exhausted, safety-stopped, superseded, candidate-forming, or unresolved with a review condition. Invalid execution is separated from negative scientific evidence.

Closeout records search history, resources, evidence exposures, artifacts, findings, limitations, and questions created. A session that finds nothing can still improve the institution if the design was valid and the searched region remains reconstructible.

## 1.6 Acceptance

The laboratory is accepted when it can run a bounded end-to-end discovery session, reproduce its attempts, prevent unauthorized evidence access, surface prior failures, generate a reviewable hypothesis contract, submit deterministic experiments, preserve opposition, and close every branch into institutional memory.

Candidate volume and historical performance are not acceptance measures. The primary measure is whether discovery becomes more informative without becoming less honest.
