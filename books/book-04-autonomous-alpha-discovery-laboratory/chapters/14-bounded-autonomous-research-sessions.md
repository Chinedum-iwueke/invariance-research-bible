# Chapter 14 — Bounded Autonomous Research Sessions

An autonomous research session is a governed execution of a scientific charter by one or more workers. Autonomy describes tactical freedom inside the charter. It does not include authority to broaden the question, consume new evidence, spend beyond budget, approve findings, or continue indefinitely.

## 14.1 Session contract

The session contract references program, question, hypothesis or exploratory objective, eligible markets and data, representation and experiment languages, tools, model profiles, search and compute budgets, protected evidence, elapsed time, stopping, required artifacts, and escalation.

It declares whether work is exploratory, confirmatory, replication, adversarial, or infrastructure. Mixed sessions separate their phases and evidence access.

## 14.2 Worker topology

Workers can specialize in retrieval, mechanism generation, representation, search, experiment execution, statistics, criticism, and synthesis. The topology records shared models, context, code, data, and incentives so apparent role diversity is not mistaken for independence.

The orchestrator schedules tasks and dependencies. It cannot change scientific contracts or resolve substantive dissent by majority vote.

> **Discovery rule B4-DR14 — The session cannot self-expand**  
> Budget, tool, data, holdout, market, environment, and authority boundaries are enforced outside the cognitive worker; exceeding them requires an attributable amendment or session closeout.

## 14.3 State and checkpointing

Session state includes task graph, attempts, artifacts, budgets, evidence exposures, pending decisions, and terminal dispositions. Checkpoints permit bounded recovery. A retry creates a new attempt and retains the failure that triggered it.

Model context is derived working memory, not the canonical session state. Restarting with another runtime must not erase attempts or permissions.

## 14.4 Tool execution

Tools receive typed inputs and scoped credentials through controlled services. Deterministic calculation and experiment services return identified artifacts. Generated code is sandboxed, resource-limited, tested, and prohibited from capital-bearing or unrestricted network action.

Tool errors, partial outputs, and cancellation have explicit states. Workers cannot treat a failed tool as successful by summarizing intended behavior.

## 14.5 Stopping and escalation

Sessions stop on success criteria, futility, invalid data, budget, protected-evidence exhaustion, safety concern, repeated failure, or elapsed time. An unresolved question can be the correct terminal result.

Escalation states the blocker, evidence, proposed next action, and least additional authority required. Lack of response does not create permission. Emergency controls favor containment and revocation.

## 14.6 Closeout

Closeout publishes the session manifest, complete material search history, artifacts, candidate contracts, invalid and negative results, resource use, dissent, limitations, and next questions. Hermes records disposition; Research Intelligence ingests memory.

Evaluation covers scientific utility, scope adherence, attempted bypass, reproducibility, failure handling, and denominator-aware candidate quality. Throughput is secondary.
