# Appendix A — Hypothesis Contract

The canonical hypothesis contract includes identity and version; question and claim type; scope; mechanism or predictive rationale; alternatives; information set and decision time; representation; target and horizon; expected observations; null or comparators; data and point-in-time policy; experiment family; metrics and uncertainty; costs and constraints; search budget; stopping; holdout policy; falsification; invalidity; amendment; closeout; and author, reviewer, and registration timestamps.

Fields reference canonical objects where possible. Missing elements are explicit and prevent execution when policy marks them required.
