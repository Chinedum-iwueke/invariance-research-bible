# Appendix C — Autonomous Session Closeout

Closeout records charter and versions, workers and shared dependencies, task graph, budgets consumed, complete material search history, evidence exposures, deterministic artifacts, candidate hypothesis contracts, invalid and negative results, dissent, unresolved questions, safety or policy events, final disposition, and required follow-up.

Every branch receives a terminal status. Omitted or inaccessible artifacts are named with reason. Closeout publication to Research Intelligence and Hermes disposition are separate authenticated events.
