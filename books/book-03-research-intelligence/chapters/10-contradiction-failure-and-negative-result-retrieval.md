# Chapter 10 — Contradiction, Failure, and Negative-Result Retrieval

Retrieval systems tend to reward similarity, popularity, and well-formed positive claims. Scientific memory needs the opposite path as well: deliberate recovery of contradiction, failed replication, invalid work, adverse operational evidence, and prior decisions not to proceed.

## 10.1 Types of opposition

Research Intelligence distinguishes:

- **direct contradiction** — incompatible claims under materially comparable scope;
- **boundary failure** — a claim fails outside or at an edge of its stated scope;
- **replication failure** — a valid study aimed at the same question yields inconsistent evidence;
- **method critique** — assumptions, estimand, measurement, or inference are challenged;
- **alternative explanation** — another account fits or predicts the observation;
- **operational contradiction** — observed system behavior conflicts with a specification or assumption.

Different populations or horizons are not automatically contradictory. The relationship record explains comparability and unresolved differences.

## 10.2 Negative and invalid work

A valid negative result can weaken a claim, bound an effect, eliminate a design, or reduce redundant search. It retains method, uncertainty, scope, and quality like any positive result.

An invalid attempt remains retrievable for engineering, contamination, and search-history purposes but does not count as a scientific null. Queries can request both while keeping their status distinct.

> **Knowledge rule B3-KR10 — Opposition is first-class**  
> Every material evidence dossier deliberately searches for applicable contradiction, failure, alternative explanation, and negative institutional history, without manufacturing false balance or treating invalid work as refutation.

## 10.3 Opposition retrieval

Query expansion uses antonyms and negation cautiously, but opposition is primarily structured: claims linked as contradicts or weakens, failed replications, adverse reviews, invalidation events, demotions, incidents, and alternative mechanisms. Citation networks and nearest-prior comparisons can expose critiques that semantic similarity misses.

An opposition budget is a minimum effort appropriate to consequence, not a quota requiring equal positive and negative sources. If no credible opposition is found, the dossier records search coverage and gaps rather than declaring consensus.

## 10.4 Failure memory

Institutional failures are indexed by question, hypothesis family, representation, data, method, code, environment, root cause, affected claim, and remediation. This permits a new proposal to retrieve not just similar titles but shared failure mechanisms.

Repeated work can still be justified by changed conditions, independent replication, improved methods, or strategic importance. The proposal states why the prior failure does not settle the new question.

## 10.5 Contradiction lifecycle

A proposed contradiction is reviewed, scoped, and linked to the claims it affects. Resolution can narrow a claim, identify a measurement difference, invalidate evidence, revise a belief, or preserve an unresolved dispute. Resolution does not delete the dissent.

Contradiction retrieval succeeds when it changes the evidence map honestly, including the conclusion that two results concern different objects.
