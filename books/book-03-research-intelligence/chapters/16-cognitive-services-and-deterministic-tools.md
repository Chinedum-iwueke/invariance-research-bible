# Chapter 16 — Cognitive Services and Deterministic Tools

Research Intelligence supports reasoning through bounded cognitive services that retrieve evidence, formalize questions, compare methods, invoke deterministic tools, and publish typed artifacts. A service can use a language model, rules, search, solvers, or several components. The model is implementation, not identity.

## 16.1 Service contracts

A cognitive-service request states role, task, evidence requirements, permitted sources and tools, context manifest, budget, output schema, uncertainty, and escalation conditions. The response identifies inputs, retrieval, tool actions, model or rule version, result, limitations, and artifact identity.

Services do not acquire decision rights from their names. A “Senior Quantitative Researcher” service can synthesize and recommend; Hermes routes approval to the accountable organization.

## 16.2 Mathematical reasoning

Mathematical support defines variables and assumptions, retrieves applicable results, formalizes the problem, and invokes symbolic or numerical tools. Solver status, feasibility, convergence, tolerances, conditioning, units, and independent checks remain visible.

A second calculation is not independent when it uses the same defective implementation. Proof-like model output remains a proposed derivation until checked at the level required by consequence.

## 16.3 Method intelligence

Statistical support begins with scientific question, estimand, sampling and dependence, censoring, selection history, uncertainty need, and decision consequence. It retrieves candidate methods, assumptions, diagnostics, alternatives, and failure modes.

It does not prescribe a fashionable test or a universal checklist. P-values, Bayesian posteriors, predictive intervals, causal estimates, and robustness analyses answer different questions.

## 16.4 Representation and ML intelligence

Representation support retrieves transforms, causal availability, leakage risks, invariance, fit/transform boundaries, stability, interpretability, and downstream compatibility. ML support first asks whether machine learning is warranted and identifies simple competitive baselines, target, horizon, dependence, calibration, cost, and operational constraints.

Books IV and VI own research and ML methods. Research Intelligence supplies evidence and records the reasoning artifact.

> **Knowledge rule B3-KR16 — Tools ground cognition**  
> Calculations, simulations, schema validation, source resolution, and governed transitions use identified deterministic tools; a cognitive runtime may coordinate and interpret them but cannot silently substitute its own output.

## 16.5 Deliberation and dissent

Specialist services publish position artifacts containing claims, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, proposal, conflicts, and decision sensitivity. Deliberation preserves minority positions. More retrieval, a calculation, an experiment, or accountable judgment resolves different conflicts; voting is not evidence integration.

## 16.6 Evaluation and replacement

Service evaluation covers grounding, citation, method fit, tool use, scope, calibration, security, escalation, reproducibility, and downstream utility. Runtime replacement requires regression evidence against versioned tasks and protected evaluations.

Memory and service identity survive replacement. A better model improves a capability; it does not become the institution’s brain by itself.
