# Chapter 19 — Milestones, Dependencies, and Definition of Done

Milestones are evidence-producing vertical slices with explicit dependencies and terminal acceptance.

## 19.1 Sequence

1. Canonical IDs, schemas, identity, policy, artifact store, and audit.
2. Data provenance and Research Intelligence MVP.
3. Trial Registry with pinned Book V integration.
4. ML registry and reproducible training.
5. Portfolio candidate and deterministic risk services.
6. Shadow MVP.
7. Execution replay and demo certification.
8. Micro-live envelope.
9. Agent orchestration over established tools.
10. Production portfolio and mature operations.

The Research Intelligence milestone first establishes source identity, rights, recovery, retrieval, and lineage. Its journal-surveillance extension then adds approved finance and trading publication watchlists, daily or weekly scheduling, acquisition observability, knowledge-agent triage, and governed routing into the relevant research laboratory. It is complete only when missed issues and connector failures are observable, technique briefs replay to source artifacts, and downstream dispositions are attributable. Technique adoption remains a separate laboratory and governance outcome.

## 19.2 Definition of done

For each slice: requirements traced; schemas versioned; implementation reviewed; tests and security passed; docs and runbooks current; telemetry and alerts active; load and failure tested; data and migration validated; rollback rehearsed; owner trained; acceptance artifacts published.

## 19.3 Dependency policy

Do not build autonomous orchestration before deterministic services, live scaling before micro-live evidence, or knowledge automation before canonical provenance. Parallel work is allowed where interfaces are stable.

> **Builder rule B10-BD19 — Milestones end in operating evidence**  
> A feature is done only when its contracts, controls, tests, observability, recovery, ownership, and intended-environment evidence are complete.
