# Chapter 12 — Micro-live Safety Envelope

Micro-live introduces real market mutation under a narrow, independently approved envelope.

## 12.1 Preconditions

Pinned release, adapter certification, reconciled account, production credentials, approved candidate, current venue rules, monitoring, staffed escalation, kill test, rollback, and signed authority.

## 12.2 Envelope

Allowlisted venue, account, instruments, order types, hours, quantity, notional, position, loss, order rate, participation, leverage, and duration. Default deny. Canary expansion requires new approval.

## 12.3 Runtime

Pre-trade risk, idempotent OMS, private stream, periodic REST reconciliation, heartbeat, alerts, freeze, cancel, reduce-only fallback, and immutable journal operate continuously.

## 12.4 Exit

Stop on completion, time, loss, discrepancy, stale state, venue incident, policy breach, or operator action. Close or transfer residual risk by plan and reconcile before evaluation.

> **Builder rule B10-BD12 — Micro-live is a bounded experiment**  
> Real mutation is limited by independently enforced scope, loss, time, state, monitoring, and kill controls.
