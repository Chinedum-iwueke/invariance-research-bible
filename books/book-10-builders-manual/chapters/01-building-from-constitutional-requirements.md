# Chapter 1 — Building from Constitutional Requirements

Implementation begins by converting Books I–IX into testable requirements, interfaces, and ownership. Architecture serves the scientific institution; it does not redefine doctrine for engineering convenience.

## 1.1 Requirement chain

Every material component links constitutional invariant, owning volume and chapter, ADR or RFC, schema or API, implementation, test, operational control, and acceptance evidence. Unknown requirements remain open rather than guessed into code.

## 1.2 Build order

Establish identity, provenance, decision authority, immutable artifacts, and deterministic execution before autonomous orchestration. Begin with vertical slices that produce real evidence: registered question, authorized experiment, validated run bundle, publication, review, and decision.

## 1.3 Boundaries

Separate scientific truth, organizational state, operational state, and derived projections. Agents, dashboards, search indexes, and model outputs never become canonical simply because they are convenient interfaces.

## 1.4 Acceptance

Each increment has functional, scientific, security, observability, recovery, and documentation criteria. “Deployed” means behavior is verified in the intended environment with rollback and owner.

> **Builder rule B10-BD01 — Trace requirements to evidence**  
> No critical service or schema is complete until its institutional requirement, enforcing implementation, test, owner, and operational evidence are linked.

## Source note

This chapter synthesizes Books I–IX and introduces no independent doctrine.
