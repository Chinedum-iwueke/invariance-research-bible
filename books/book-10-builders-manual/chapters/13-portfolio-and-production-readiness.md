# Chapter 13 — Portfolio and Production Readiness

Production readiness joins scientific admission, portfolio feasibility, execution evidence, and platform operation without collapsing their gates.

## 13.1 Readiness packet

Include candidate and model versions, evidence dossier, allocation and limits, cost and capacity, dependency and stress, execution parity, environment qualification, data and service SLOs, monitoring, incident history, owners, rollback, decision, conditions, and expiry.

## 13.2 State

Deployment, scientific, portfolio, and operational states are separate. A service can be healthy while a candidate is quarantined; a valid candidate can be operationally unready.

## 13.3 Rehearsal

Run game days for data loss, stale state, model failure, venue outage, reconciliation mismatch, duplicate events, margin stress, and rollback. Verify humans and systems follow the runbook.

## 13.4 Acceptance

The portfolio can be reconstructed, constraints replayed, orders reconciled, incidents contained, and risk reduced under degraded dependencies.

> **Builder rule B10-BD13 — Production readiness is conjunctive**  
> Scientific, portfolio, execution, security, operational, and authority gates must all be current; strength in one cannot compensate for failure in another.
