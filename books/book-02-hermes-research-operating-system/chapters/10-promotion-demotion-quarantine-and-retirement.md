# Chapter 10 — Promotion, Demotion, Quarantine, and Retirement

Promotion is an authorization to treat a candidate as having reached a defined maturity. It is not a declaration that the candidate is true, profitable, or safe in every environment. Hermes separates maturity decisions from epistemic assessment, environment execution, and operational health.

## 10.1 Candidate maturity

The canonical maturity states are:

- **registered** — candidate identity and originating claim exist;
- **screened** — minimum validity and relevance checks passed;
- **replicated** — required independent or new-data checks are satisfied within scope;
- **robust** — specified perturbation and alternative tests are satisfied;
- **portfolio eligible** — portfolio authority may consider allocation;
- **production eligible** — governance and production requirements permit capital-bearing operation;
- **degraded** — evidence or readiness has weakened but bounded use may remain;
- **quarantined** — use is prohibited pending investigation or remediation;
- **retired** — authorized use has ended.

The sequence is a controlled default. A candidate cannot become production eligible before portfolio admission and applicable risk authorization. Entry into shadow, demo, micro-live, or production is recorded separately as environment evidence.

## 10.2 Transition records

Every transition cites the candidate version, prior and new state, applicable policy, evidence dossier, independent review, accountable authority, veto clearance, conditions, limits, expiry, monitoring, and rollback trigger.

Evidence requirements are claim- and candidate-specific. A venue-specific execution improvement and a market-general predictive claim do not share one universal gate checklist. Policy defines the minimum; the decision record explains additional judgment.

> **Operating rule B2-OR10 — Promotion is bounded authorization**  
> A maturity transition grants only the permissions named by its target state, conditions, environment, and effective interval; it does not confer authority by analogy.

## 10.3 Portfolio eligibility

Portfolio eligibility means the candidate has an evidence package suitable for consideration by portfolio and risk authority. It does not guarantee allocation. The package includes expected incremental contribution, uncertainty, dependence, costs, capacity, implementation status, failure modes, monitoring, and unresolved dissent.

Book VII owns allocation and risk budgets. Hermes records the admission decision, conditions, expiry, and relationship to candidate maturity.

## 10.4 Production eligibility

Production eligibility requires an admitted portfolio role, assigned risk bounds, exercised operational controls, reconciliation, incident procedures, signed artifacts, and Production acceptance. Favorable demo or micro-live outcomes are relevant evidence but never sufficient alone.

The production version is fixed. A semantic change to representation, model, decision logic, risk, execution, or dependency triggers change classification and the required reevaluation.

## 10.5 Demotion and quarantine

Demotion responds to weakened evidence, expired approval, degraded costs, changed dependence, operational defects, missing monitoring, or policy change. It states which permissions cease and whether existing exposure must be reduced or closed.

Quarantine is immediate isolation when integrity, safety, provenance, security, or validity is uncertain. It favors preventing further use while evidence is preserved. Quarantine does not establish guilt or scientific rejection; investigation determines disposition.

Emergency authority can quarantine or reduce risk without waiting for ordinary review. Expansion and re-entry require affirmative authorization.

## 10.6 Retirement and reinstatement

Retirement ends authorized use and records reason, final state, affected deployments, evidence, unresolved obligations, and knowledge return. Artifacts remain retrievable under retention policy. A retired candidate does not return to use by clearing a flag; reinstatement creates a new review against current evidence, implementation, costs, and policy.

Demotion and retirement are learning events, not administrative cleanup. The institution studies why earlier expectations no longer justified authority.
