# Chapter 1 — From Constitution to Operation

Book I states what Invariance Research refuses to trade away: claim discipline, separated authority, durable memory, explicit representation, bounded autonomy, and the distinction between knowledge and profit. Those commitments become operational only when they constrain actual decisions, permissions, artifacts, and state transitions.

Hermes is the governed research operating system and organization through which those constraints become repeatable work. It does not replace scientific judgment. It makes judgment attributable, limits what any participant may do alone, and preserves the evidence required to evaluate what happened.

## 1.1 The control chain

An institutional commitment moves through five forms:

1. **Doctrine** states the controlled obligation or boundary.
2. **Policy** identifies the actors, scope, conditions, exceptions, and accountable authority.
3. **Control** prevents, permits, detects, or records a consequential action.
4. **Artifact** preserves the inputs, outputs, rationale, and identity of the action.
5. **Assurance evidence** demonstrates whether the policy and control operated as intended.

The chain prevents two failures. Doctrine without controls becomes aspiration. Controls without doctrine become arbitrary machinery whose purpose cannot be reviewed.

For example, the doctrine that no proposer promotes its own work becomes a policy separating proposal, evaluation, and promotion roles. Permissions prevent the proposer from executing the promotion transition. A signed decision record identifies the evaluator, evidence package, decision, conditions, and expiry. An assurance test attempts the prohibited transition and verifies that it is denied and audited.

> **Operating rule B2-OR01 — Traceable control**  
> Every material Hermes control links to a canonical obligation, an accountable owner, an observable enforcement point, and evidence by which its operation can be tested.

## 1.2 Policy is versioned institutional behavior

Policies state who may decide, what resources are in scope, which evidence is required, what vetoes apply, how long authorization remains valid, and what happens under exception or failure. A policy has an identity, version, effective interval, owner, approval, and supersession history.

A text file can express policy, but publication alone does not enforce it. Some policies operate through training and review; others require software controls, credentials, workflow gates, or deterministic risk services. The enforcement mode follows consequence. A naming convention does not require the same machinery as a capital limit.

Policy changes do not rewrite prior decisions. A transition is evaluated under the policy version effective at the decision time. Later reviewers can reconstruct both the rule and its application.

## 1.3 Typed artifacts carry work

Hermes coordinates through typed, versioned artifacts rather than private context. A research question, hypothesis, trial registration, dataset reference, experiment specification, run, evidence dossier, validation review, exception, promotion decision, and incident are distinct objects with distinct authority.

Typing does not mean every thought becomes a schema. It means consequential boundaries have explicit contracts. The contract identifies required fields, lineage, state, allowed transitions, and validation. Human-readable narrative remains essential for mechanisms, objections, limitations, and decisions that cannot be reduced to fields.

Artifacts are immutable where historical identity matters and append-only where state evolves. Corrections link rather than overwrite. Content digests establish artifact identity; version identifiers establish schema or controlled revision. These functions remain separate.

## 1.4 Decisions, actions, and observations

Hermes distinguishes a decision from its execution and observed outcome. A committee can authorize shadow observation; a scheduler can start it; a service can record signals; later evidence can show whether expectations held. Collapsing these events makes it impossible to determine whether a poor outcome arose from judgment, execution, or environment.

Every material decision states the question before the authority, evidence considered, alternatives, dissent, applicable policy, conditions, expiry, and required follow-up. The packet should be sufficient for substantive approval, not ceremonial signature.

## 1.5 Determinism and cognition

Model runtimes and agents may synthesize evidence, generate hypotheses, propose designs, and criticize results. They are not authoritative for calculations, simulation, risk verdicts, permissions, or state transitions. Those functions use versioned deterministic services whose inputs and outputs can be reproduced or audited.

Deterministic does not mean infallible. Code can contain defects and specifications can be wrong. Determinism makes behavior inspectable and testable. Model assistance can improve a design, but it cannot become an unrecorded alternative control path.

## 1.6 Failure containment

Controls are designed for expected failure: unavailable services, invalid artifacts, expired approvals, ambiguous identity, budget exhaustion, data contamination, and attempted privilege escalation. Failure defaults depend on consequence. A missing optional retrieval may allow a low-risk exploratory task to continue with a warning; an unavailable risk verdict cannot permit a capital-bearing order.

Retries create new attributable attempts. They do not erase the original error or silently reuse a partially completed transition. Idempotency protects against duplicate effects while preserving evidence that repeated requests occurred.

## 1.7 Operation returns to knowledge

Hermes is not complete when a workflow finishes. The resulting artifacts, decisions, failures, exceptions, and outcomes must return to Research Intelligence with provenance and applicable access controls. Book III owns the memory architecture; Book II owns the obligation and handoff.

This return closes the constitutional loop. Operation becomes institutional learning only when future work can retrieve the relevant episode and understand how it should change the next decision.
