# Chapter 13 — Laboratory and Production Boundaries

Research evidence and capital-bearing operation share representations, models, decision rules, and market interfaces, but they do not share authority. Hermes governs the boundary so that experimental flexibility cannot become an unreviewed production path and production urgency cannot rewrite the experiment.

## 13.1 The laboratory contract

Bulletproof BT is the deterministic experimental laboratory. A governed request supplies registered identities, data contracts, code and environment versions, permitted search, cost and execution assumptions, budgets, and required artifacts. The laboratory executes and reports; it does not grant scientific validity or candidate maturity.

Experimental code can be provisional, but primary evidence must remain reproducible and attributable. Notebook output or an agent summary can aid exploration without becoming the authoritative run artifact.

## 13.2 The production contract

Production accepts only signed, production-eligible packages whose candidate, representation, model, decision logic, risk, execution, configuration, monitoring, dependencies, and rollback identities are fixed. It verifies the package rather than rebuilding it from research files.

Configuration is part of behavior. A parameter, universe, venue route, feature version, or risk limit change receives classification and authority proportionate to its semantic effect. Environment variables and manual edits cannot become an invisible promotion channel.

> **Operating rule B2-OR13 — No implicit production path**  
> No research artifact, model output, notebook, or laboratory process may submit capital-bearing orders unless it has crossed the explicit production package, admission, risk, and operational controls.

## 13.3 Data and time boundaries

Research and production resolve data through versioned contracts with event, receive, and availability semantics. Historical runs preserve point-in-time universes and corrections. Production records what was actually available to each decision.

Parity means shared semantics and tested transformations, not necessarily identical infrastructure. Differences are documented and exercised through replay and demo before capital exposure.

## 13.4 External constraints

Venue, broker, account, prop-firm, legal, and jurisdictional rules are versioned constraint overlays. They can veto or narrow action but are not alpha logic. Authority records the source, effective date, account or venue scope, and uncertainty of interpretation.

## 13.5 Handoff and feedback

Production handoff includes owners, runbooks, reconciliation, monitoring, incident thresholds, risk limits, and expiry. Production returns fills, rejections, latency, costs, state, incidents, and outcome records to Research Intelligence. A laboratory assumption contradicted in operation becomes a registered issue, not a silent patch.
