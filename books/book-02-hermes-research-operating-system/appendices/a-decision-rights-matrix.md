# Appendix A — Decision-Rights Matrix

This matrix establishes default organizational accountability. Policy may narrow a right or add vetoes; it cannot silently transfer it.

| Decision | Accountable organization | Required contributions | Independent vetoes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research-program charter | Research | Knowledge, Engineering, Governance | Governance for policy or protected-data failure |
| Trial and holdout access | Governance | Research, Knowledge | Security/data authority |
| Experiment execution | Engineering | Research specification | Governance for authorization; Engineering for validity |
| Evidence admissibility | Governance | Independent evaluator, Engineering, Knowledge | Scientific-validity or integrity veto |
| Candidate maturity | Governance | Research, evaluation, Knowledge | Risk, security, or operational readiness as applicable |
| Portfolio admission | Governance | Research and Book VII portfolio authority | Risk authority |
| Production eligibility | Production | Governance, Engineering, portfolio/risk authority | Security, risk, operational readiness |
| Capital allocation | Production | Book VII portfolio and risk authorities | Hard risk and mandate constraints |
| Emergency halt or quarantine | Production or Governance within scope | Immediate evidence; retrospective review | None against risk reduction within delegated bounds |
| Canonical knowledge status | Knowledge | Domain review and provenance | Constitutional or security authority as applicable |
| Policy exception | Governance | Affected owner, security/risk review | Constitutional boundary |

Concrete deployments must map named roles and services to these organizations, define limits and expiry, and test both permitted and denied actions.
