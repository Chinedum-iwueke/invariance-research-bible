The original program does not define a separate Phase 6 deliverable; advanced
orchestration research begins at Part VII. The roadmap's Stage 6 is covered by the
second-year original-systems-architecture trajectory.

## What remains empirical

Documentation completion does not demonstrate founder mastery or architectural
superiority. Those claims require completed diagnostics, controlled benchmarks,
reproducible experiment records, and design defenses. The experiment registry begins
in [`experiments/README.md`](experiments/README.md).
