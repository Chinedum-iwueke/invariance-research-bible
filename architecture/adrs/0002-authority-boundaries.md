# ADR-0002: Authority boundaries

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-08

## Context

Language models can synthesize and propose, but simulation, calculation, risk, state transition, and evidentiary authority require reproducible controls.

## Decision

Research Intelligence is the institutional brain, corpus, and durable memory. Hermes is the governed operating system and organization. Bulletproof BT is the deterministic experimental laboratory. Model runtimes are replaceable. Deterministic services are authoritative for calculations and controlled state transitions. Exploration, evaluation, governance, and promotion authority remain separated.

## Consequences

No agent may promote its own work. Model output is not primary evidence. Every consequential transition uses versioned contracts and returns its records to Research Intelligence.
