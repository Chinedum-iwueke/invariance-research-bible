# ADR-0001: Ten-volume canonical architecture

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-08

## Context

Founder’s Editions and prior planning material contain overlapping and differently ordered scopes. A stable series dependency is required before migration.

## Decision

Adopt the ten-volume sequence listed in the root README. The dependency is why, operate, think, discover, experiment, learn, allocate, execute, staff, build.

## Consequences

Books I–III remain constitutional dependencies but undergo Version 2 migration. Material whose authoritative home moved is preserved through a local bridge and relocated treatment. Later books cannot silently redefine earlier doctrine.
