# ADR-0003: Concept ownership and cross-reference policy

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-08

## Context

The Founder’s Editions intentionally developed related ideas from several perspectives, but a ten-volume reference series requires a single authoritative treatment for each major concept.

## Decision

Adopt the concept ownership map in `registers/CONCEPT_OWNERSHIP.md`. Non-owning books state only the minimum local dependency, interface, or consequence and cross-reference the authoritative chapter.

## Consequences

Migration may split chapters. Repetition is removed only after its unique argument or nuance is preserved at the authoritative location.
