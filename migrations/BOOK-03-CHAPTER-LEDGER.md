## Part I–III evidence notes

### Part I — Mandate and Constitutional Cognition

The opening chapters state the canonical distinction clearly: Research Intelligence is the durable cognitive substrate and memory through which Hermes retrieves evidence, reconstructs experience, reasons with tools, forms questions, and records belief; it is neither a model nor a chat interface. The four-plane decomposition—reasoning kernel, institutional substrate, deterministic analytical plane, and control plane—is useful as a logical view, but the reasoning kernel need not always be an LLM. Model runtimes remain replaceable, including non-generative or rule-based components.

Constitutional context is immutable within a bounded task, while canonical doctrine itself changes only through explicit governance. “Policy compilation” is a target capability and cannot make ambiguous prose automatically executable; unresolved conflicts require escalation and an auditable interpretation. The four universal output states—ACCEPT, REVISE, REJECT, ESCALATE—are decision outcomes, not a substitute for domain-specific states such as ingestion status, evidence validity, or access denial.

Claim discipline is foundational. Mathematical identity, formal theorem, external empirical claim, internal finding, model output, agent inference, institutional belief, dispute, unknown, and retired claim require different evidence and semantics. A theorem is not automatically sample-independent if its premises model empirical objects. Confidence does not mechanically “inherit” from evidence; it is a typed, scoped assessment with an explicit method, validity interval, and reviewer. Context assembly should record selected items and retrieval decisions while avoiding storage of secrets or unnecessary personal material.

### Part II — Knowledge Architecture and Source Hierarchy

The Founder’s Edition describes one library with logical collections rather than isolated knowledge silos. Version 2 adopts the later four knowledge classes—Constitutional, Scientific, Institutional, and Operational Knowledge—and treats semantic, episodic, procedural, working, executive, and reflective memory as functional forms rather than competing collections. Authority remains contextual: the Bible governs institutional policy, primary science governs external claims, run artifacts govern internal empirical results, and production records govern observed operation.

Every source receives stable identity, edition/version, checksum, provenance, rights and access metadata, ingestion version, and extraction-quality measures. Duplicate editions are linked rather than silently merged. The knowledge model separates concepts, claims, evidence, assumptions, methods, variables, datasets, artifacts, decisions, and contradictions. A graph edge is a derived assertion with its own provenance and review state, not structural truth.

Curatorial governance must preserve the separation between the Librarian, who owns recovery and indexing quality, and the Canon Curator, who evaluates authority and canonical status. Neither role is allowed to upgrade a source’s claims through metadata alone. Source ranking is claim- and context-specific rather than a universal prestige score.

### Part III — Ingestion, Parsing, and Citation Preservation

The ingestion architecture correctly begins with immutable source capture and checksum, then recovery, structural understanding, object extraction, quality evaluation, indexing, and publication. Born-digital and scanned sources require different recovery paths. Page images and coordinates remain provenance even when extracted text is excellent; OCR, layout, equation, table, figure, citation, and metadata confidence remain separate.

PDF recovery preserves reading order, headings, footnotes, captions, equations, tables, marginalia, and page geometry. Equations retain source images, normalized expressions when confidence permits, symbol definitions and local scope, assumptions, derivation links, and validation state. Dimensional consistency is a useful check but does not prove transcription or mathematics correct. Tables and figures retain units, exclusions, axes, legends, panels, captions, notes, and adjacent explanation. A bibliography establishes a citation relationship, not support for the citing author’s interpretation.

“Chunking” is an implementation term that can encourage arbitrary token windows. Version 2 centers semantic retrieval units in the reconstructed hierarchy: work, edition, part, chapter, section, definition, theorem, proof, example, equation, table, figure, footnote, and reference. Small spans retain parent, sibling, continuation, caveat, and asset links; deep-reading mode retrieves the necessary larger structure. No universal overlap or token policy is canonical.

## Part IV–VI evidence notes

### Part IV — Retrieval and Evidence Dossiers

Query planning begins from a decision and an evidence-needs model rather than one semantic query. The proposed lanes—theoretical, methodological, external empirical, internal, contradictory, operational, and novelty—are useful defaults, not mandatory empty slots for every question. Planning must not encode the desired conclusion, and retrieval gaps remain visible.

Hybrid retrieval assigns distinct roles to lexical, semantic, structured, citation, formula, graph, and experiment search. Score fusion requires calibration or rank-based methods appropriate to each index; dense similarity never overrules exact identifiers or source coordinates. Reranking uses scope, authority, support, diversity, contradiction, and coverage. Opposition budgets cannot become false-balance quotas, and recency is relevant only to mutable claims.

Contradiction is typed as direct incompatibility, boundary failure, method critique, replication failure, or alternative explanation. Different scope is not contradiction. Negative results receive equal discoverability but retain quality signals; an invalid failed run is not equivalent to a valid null finding.

An evidence dossier is a decision-specific scientific briefing containing normalized claims, provenance, scope, methods, assumptions, supporting and opposing evidence, institutional history, unknowns, risks, and clearly labeled inference. “Fixed schema” should mean a stable extensible envelope, not identical content for every domain. Citation validation checks support strength, coordinates, quotation fidelity, and scope. Replay preserves the retrieval and transformation manifest, but a later index upgrade need not reproduce old ranks unless the old index snapshot or immutable candidate set is retained. Replayability concerns the recorded version, not forced immobility of current retrieval.

### Part V — Institutional Memory and Scientific Lineage

Research episode memory records the question, prior position, dossier, alternatives, methods, assumptions, experiments, criticism, decision, surprise, lesson, and new questions while preserving primary artifacts. Rationales must be recorded contemporaneously; reconstructed explanations are labeled as retrospective inference.

The Bulletproof BT memory chapter proposes comparable performance, signal, cost, robustness, and decision objects. These are target integration requirements, not verified current outputs. No field or artifact can be described as implemented until the pinned Book V audit. Research Intelligence should ingest primary run bundles and publish derived comparisons with producer/schema compatibility checks; it never turns screenshots or summaries into primary evidence.

Trial Registry integration supplies semantic duplicate detection, family resolution, selection events, result-view exposure, and holdout contamination records. A related-trial count is insufficient for adjustment and need not appear in every unrelated dossier; the relevant adaptive search history and dependence structure must be accessible. Prompt variations count when they can influence candidate selection, not merely because text changed.

Scientific lineage links sources and observations through datasets, representations, methods, code, experiment specifications, runs, reviews, decisions, releases, and operational outcomes. Content addressing and signatures are target mechanisms; stable identity, version, digest, authority, and supersession are the invariant requirements. Immutability applies to accepted historical records; privacy, security, retention, and lawful deletion may require tombstones or cryptographic erasure rather than indefinite accessibility.

Institutional belief is a scoped, versioned judgment linked to evidence for and against, uncertainty method, owner, review date, and invalidation conditions. Calling every belief a “confidence-weighted prior” overstates Bayesian formality. Confidence may be qualitative, frequentist, Bayesian, predictive, or decision-calibrated and must be typed. Calibration compares like judgments against defined outcomes; “later survival” is not a universal ground-truth target.

### Part VI — Cognitive Graph Architecture

The Founder’s Edition’s strongest graph decision is that fourteen named graphs are rebuildable, access-controlled projections over one canonical evidence and identity core—not independent truth stores. Nodes and edges carry type, scope, provenance, validity, derivation, and review state. Observed edges, deterministic derivations, probabilistic estimates, and agent-proposed inferences remain visually and semantically distinct.

Knowledge, Method, and Representation Graphs answer different questions but share canonical objects. The Method Graph must encode problem class, assumptions, diagnostics, alternatives, software evidence, and failure modes rather than become an algorithm catalog. Representation value includes information preservation, causal availability, stability, interpretability, cost, and downstream use—not predictive performance alone.

Question, Curiosity, and Opportunity views separate recorded surprise, open inquiry, and governed prioritization. An anomaly never automatically becomes a hypothesis or trade. Expected information gain, feasibility, novelty, cost, portfolio relevance, and risk are uncertain assessments with provenance, not objective graph properties.

Confidence, Uncertainty, and Information views require particularly careful semantics. Unknown unknowns cannot be enumerated or assigned convenient probabilities. “Information” may mean Shannon information, predictive increment, novelty, evidence contribution, or institutional usefulness; each edge must state its definition and estimator. Feature importance is not information, and uncertainty reduction is not automatically economic value.

Memory, Learning, and Evolution views separate historical episodes, changes in institutional understanding, and changes in the environment. Alpha and Mechanism views likewise separate predictive relationships from explanatory or causal accounts. The Book I audit’s typed distinction among predictive edge, realizable alpha, and realized active return applies here. “Execution alpha” should be represented as an execution advantage with its own evidence rather than forced into a price-prediction graph.

Graph reasoning may propose paths, analogies, gaps, and candidate explanations; centrality, connectivity, and multi-hop plausibility never establish scientific importance or belief. Every derived edge identifies its rule or model and expires or is superseded under review. Permissions propagate from source objects through projections, query results, counts, embeddings, and neighborhood metadata to prevent inference leaks.

## Part VII–IX evidence notes

### Part VII — Discovery Cognition and Research Agendas

These chapters describe consumers of Research Intelligence more than its owned internals. A governed hypothesis is assembled from a registered question, scoped claim, mechanism or predictive rationale, representation, target, horizon, null or benchmark, alternative explanations, expected observations, falsification conditions, costs, selection family, stopping rules, and evidence plan. Research Intelligence retrieves sources and prior institutional work and stores the resulting objects; Book IV owns generation, registration-ready contracts, search, and falsification.

Concept synthesis and analogy are valuable discovery services when they map premises, structure, transformations, and failure conditions rather than vocabulary. “Concept Synthesis Engine” and “Concept Engineer” are target roles, not current systems. A proposed abstraction needs a definition, boundaries, relationships to existing concepts, examples and counterexamples, testable utility, and governance before it enters controlled terminology. Metaphor never establishes mathematical equivalence.

An anomaly is a scoped deviation from a defined expectation with data availability, effect magnitude, dependence, search history, and artifact alternatives recorded. Temporal persistence and plausible mechanism are useful investigation criteria but not universal prerequisites: transient anomalies and mechanism-unknown deviations can be scientifically important. Detection, investigation, hypothesis registration, and trading remain separate states.

Novelty is assessed across question, claim, mechanism, representation, method, data, target, experiment, operational effect, and portfolio exposure. It is never inferred from wording alone. A novelty report identifies nearest prior work and material differences; it does not grant scientific or economic value. Return correlation alone cannot establish portfolio novelty.

The Continuous Research Agenda belongs to Book IV under Book II’s authority. Research Intelligence supplies open questions, literature changes, prior failures, anomalies, experiment outcomes, incidents, degradation, and corpus gaps. Expected information gain, novelty, feasibility, urgency, diversification, and cost are uncertain decision inputs. Research Intelligence does not allocate its own compute or promote its own agenda.

### Part VIII — Mathematical, Statistical, and ML Intelligence

Book III should define cognitive-service contracts: retrieve applicable mathematics and methods, identify assumptions and definitions, assemble relevant internal and external evidence, invoke deterministic tools, and publish verified artifacts. It should not become a compressed methods textbook.

The mathematical pipeline need not begin with an LLM. A reasoning runtime may formalize a question, but symbolic algebra, numerical calculation, optimization, simulation, dimensional checks, solver status, feasibility, bounds, numerical stability, and independent verification belong to deterministic tools. Reproducibility requires inputs, code, environment, tolerances, outputs, and diagnostics; a second calculation is not independent if it shares the same implementation defect.

Statistical method selection begins with a scientific question, estimand, data-generating and sampling structure, dependence, censoring, selection history, and decision consequences. The listed methods—information coefficient, survival analysis, panels, hierarchical models, Deflated Sharpe Ratio, Probability of Backtest Overfitting, false-discovery control, nested evaluation—are examples requiring primary sources and applicability checks. They are not mandatory or interchangeable. Economic magnitude does not replace uncertainty, and p-values do not replace effect size, design validity, or cost feasibility.

Representation intelligence supplies evidence about transforms, information preservation, stationarity, causal availability, leakage, stability, compatibility, interpretability, and downstream use. “Minimum transformation” is a useful simplicity preference, not a universal optimum. Learned representations remain versioned hypotheses with fit/transform boundaries and prospective evaluation. Book VI owns their construction and model-specific validation.

ML method intelligence correctly begins by deciding whether ML is warranted and requires simple, competitive baselines. Problem classification must include the decision, target, horizon, sample formation, label availability, dependence, costs, uncertainty needs, and operational constraints. Offline accuracy is insufficient. Book VI owns model families, labels, calibration, representation learning, drift, and offline reinforcement learning.

The Founder’s Edition wisely places reinforcement learning downstream of validated signals, costs, risk, and environment checks and warns about simulator exploitation. Its broad preference for predicting trade quality, risk, survivability, and cost before direction is strategic guidance, not a prohibition on directional targets. `label_end_time`, purge, and embargo are applicable temporal-leakage controls, not universal remedies for every label or split.

### Part IX — Senior Quant and Specialist Cognition

The Senior Quantitative Researcher is a target research-direction role, not a super-agent or present capability. It requests dossiers, formalizes questions, compares mechanisms and alternatives, commissions experiments and criticism, and synthesizes evidence without acquiring promotion authority. “Confidence later scored against survival” needs a defined forecast, target event, horizon, censoring rule, and calibration method.

Specialists are bounded cognitive services with role-specific evidence, tools, permissions, and output contracts. They need not all be agents or all use different models. Genuine independence requires attention to shared context, runtime, code, data, prompts, incentives, and correlated error. Consensus voting does not integrate evidence.

Deliberation produces versioned position artifacts: claims, evidence, assumptions, uncertainty, objectives, proposed actions, conflicts, and decision sensitivities. Dissent remains linked to the final decision with revisit conditions. Additional retrieval, deterministic calculation, experiment, or accountable authority resolves different conflict types; an orchestrator may summarize but cannot erase unresolved disagreement.

## Part X–XII evidence notes

### Part X — Services, Data Contracts, and Deployment

Research Intelligence requires bounded logical capabilities for ingestion, retrieval, memory, graph projections, reasoning coordination, and evaluation. Whether these become separate network services, modules, or processes is a Book X implementation decision. Expensive work should be bounded, resumable where feasible, idempotent, attributable, and explicit about cancellation and terminal status; not every operation needs asynchronous infrastructure.

APIs and schemas require stable identity, version negotiation, validation, provenance, policy metadata, correlation and causation identifiers, explicit error states, retry classification, and safe diagnostic detail. An append-only event is a historical assertion that an actor or service emitted—not necessarily an objectively true fact. Corrections and supersessions are new events. Event envelopes must minimize personal, secret, sealed, or licensed data.

PostgreSQL, pgvector, object storage, lexical indexes, and caches form a plausible initial target stack, not canonical technology. The durable principles are an authoritative transactional catalog, immutable or versioned primary objects, derived and rebuildable indexes, content identity, access-policy inheritance, backups, and replay manifests. Embedding models and indexes are versioned; old decisions use retained snapshots or candidate manifests.

Mac/VM1/VM2 is superseded as a physical topology. Logical development, evidence, control, evaluation, and production boundaries remain, with least privilege and signed promotion. Markdown is canonical for the Bible, but diagrams need not be code-generated; Mermaid or other source-controlled representations must be reviewable. Separating repositories does not by itself establish permissions or release integrity.

### Part XI — Governance, Security, and Adversarial Defense

Permissions are scoped by identity, task, purpose, resource, action, environment, and time. Agents do not inherit authority from prompt ancestry. Reviewer isolation protects independent adjudication, but a frozen package needs a governed request-for-context path and cannot conceal relevant omissions. Permissions extend to metadata, embeddings, caches, graph neighborhoods, aggregate counts, exports, and derived artifacts.

The sealed-holdout chapter mixes protected historical partitions with prospective environments. Development, internal validation, and sealed historical holdouts are data-access classes; shadow and micro-live are validation environments. Execution replay and demo are missing and must be added to the environment model. Holdout gates should provide prespecified bounded results; query timing, sizes, filenames, cache keys, and error behavior must not leak partition information. Every exposure that can influence adaptation is recorded.

Corpus content remains untrusted evidence and cannot alter system policy. Prompt injection, metadata instructions, active document content, poisoned sources, and exfiltration attempts require channel separation, sandboxing, authentication, provenance, anomaly detection, and escalation. “Instruction stripping” must not corrupt the preserved source; sanitization creates a derived view while the raw source remains protected evidence. Source reputation informs review but never suppresses legitimate dissent automatically.

Privacy and confidentiality controls include classification, purpose limitation, minimization, rights review, encryption, regional/storage requirements, field-level access, redaction, retention schedules, legal hold, deletion propagation, and certificates. Derived indexes inherit restrictions. Institutional memory is not authorization to retain everything indefinitely or train/fine-tune models on protected content.

Red-team work tests laboratory leakage, registry manipulation, simulator exploitation, privilege escalation, approval spoofing, stale credentials, replay behavior, poisoning, and evidence corruption—not refusal style alone. Traps and canaries require explicit authorization, containment, cleanup, and protection against contaminating ordinary evaluation or research. Findings become regression cases with owners and deadlines.

### Part XII — Evaluation, Operations, and Evolution

Retrieval evaluation measures source-, section-, page-, object-, and claim-level recall, utility, support, contradiction coverage, diversity, latency, and cost by source and domain. Gold sets require independent judgments, adjudication, uncertainty, versioning, contamination controls, and protected production-like queries. System labels and exact prompts must not leak to judges or development contexts.

Reasoning evaluation scores intermediate artifacts: problem formulation, estimand, horizon, data availability, method fit, assumptions, deterministic calculations, falsification design, leakage detection, evidence use, and decision sensitivity. “Tests capable of killing the hypothesis” means discriminating power under plausible alternatives, not a guarantee of rejection. Expert disagreement is recorded and adjudicated rather than averaged without a rubric.

Novelty requires nearest-prior analysis. Calibration requires a clearly defined forecast probability, event, horizon, censoring rule, scoring rule, and claim stratum; Brier and log scores do not apply to untyped qualitative confidence. “Later survival” is not a universal outcome. Institutional learning evaluation tests retrieval of prior work, avoidance of unjustified repetition, correct use of failures, and traceable changes to subsequent tasks; fewer repeated errors can also reflect reduced activity and needs exposure-adjusted interpretation.

Operational service levels cover evidence freshness, catalog and object availability, schema/citation/lineage/permission consistency, degraded behavior, queue budgets, storage growth, backup completeness, restore tests, index reconstruction, and semantic equivalence checks. A graph rebuild can alter derived rankings while remaining semantically correct; version and compare results rather than demand byte identity.

The RI-0–RI-5 phases are superseded by Book X’s integrated roadmap but preserve a sound order: identities and benchmarks; document recovery and retrieval; experiment memory and lineage; dossiers and tools; closed-loop research; then proactive discovery. Research Intelligence never gains production deployment authority.

Research Intelligence v1 should be accepted through a governed demonstration that recovers and cites evidence, surfaces contradiction and gaps, retrieves relevant prior work and failures, preserves claim types, compiles a dossier, supports a registration-ready hypothesis, ingests resulting experiment artifacts, records a scoped belief/decision update, and makes the episode available to a later task. It need not autonomously produce a valuable research program or prove that its questions are novel in an absolute sense. Model replacement must not rewrite memory or bypass system regression evaluation.

## Book III audit conclusion

All sixty chapters and appendices have received dispositions and evidence review. Book III’s durable contribution is the architecture of trustworthy institutional cognition: contextual authority, scientific document recovery, typed evidence, retrieval, dossiers, memory, lineage, belief records, graph projections, cognitive-service boundaries, corpus defense, and evaluation. Version 2 will consolidate repeated capability-card boilerplate, retain domain contracts, remove implementation overclaims, and relocate discovery, ML, agent construction, and physical deployment without reducing their Research Intelligence interfaces.
