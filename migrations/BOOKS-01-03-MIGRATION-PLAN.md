# Books I–III Version 2 Migration Plan

## Governing method

Migration preserves substantive doctrine and developed reasoning while changing ownership, structure, and prose where necessary. Each Founder’s Edition chapter receives a disposition: **retain**, **revise**, **split**, **relocate**, **supersede**, or **archive**. Relocation preserves a concise bridge in the source volume and assigns full treatment to the authoritative later book. No conflict is silently harmonized.

## Book I

Retain the constitutional arc from institutional purpose through scientific humility and the invariants. Consolidate recurring formulations of discovery, knowledge compounding, and weak edges without flattening their arguments. Convert operating prescriptions about daily workflow, detailed cognitive architecture, and portfolios into constitutional principles with cross-references to Books II, III, and VII. Distinguish doctrine from externally testable claims about markets and information.

## Book II

Retain operating mandate, organizational design, decision rights, separation of duties, governed research lifecycle, trial registration, promotion state, audit, security, cadence, incidents, and retirement. Narrow Research Intelligence chapters to the operating contract with Book III. Narrow agent chapters to roles and authority, relocating construction and runtime detail to Book IX. Narrow portfolio, data, topology, and implementation chapters to organizational requirements and cross-references.

## Book III

Retain constitutional reasoning support, knowledge classes, provenance, scientific document recovery, semantic structure, retrieval, evidence dossiers, institutional memory, lineage, belief records, graph projections, citation validation, corpus security, and evaluation. Relocate discovery methods to Book IV, ML methods to Book VI, agent implementation to Book IX, and deployment/repository construction to Book X. Preserve the Research Intelligence interfaces to each relocated domain.

## Migration evidence

Each revised chapter will include a source note in its front matter during review. A book-level migration ledger will list Founder’s Edition pages, disposition, destination, substantive changes, conflicts resolved, and ADRs applied. Source notes may be removed from reading prose only after the ledger is complete.
